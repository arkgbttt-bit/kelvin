Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

The Service uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. When the account owner authorizes the Service, we receive an access token and basic profile information (user.info.basic) solely to upload draft videos (video.upload) to that account.

We store only the authorization tokens on our own server. We do not sell, share or publish this data, and we do not collect data from other TikTok users. Tokens can be deleted on request, and you can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions).

Contact: through the Kelvin Telegram bot.
